# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People studying technical English through repository reading notes in focused 20–40 minute sessions.

## Product Purpose

English Reading Notes turns technical repository analysis into a calm, readable study experience where English passages lead and Chinese explanations support comprehension.

## Brand Personality

Calm, editorial, focused.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic MkDocs documentation density, dashboard widgets, oversized display headings, decorative gradients, and noisy visual effects.

## Design Principles

- English-first reading flow.
- Distinct sentence-level study units.
- Quiet, useful hierarchy over decoration.
- Static, accessible, responsive pages.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep readable contrast, comfortable mixed English/Chinese typography, responsive cards, keyboard-friendly navigation, and reduced-motion support.
